2. Implementation check

I wrote a vectorised implementation (one tensor contraction instead of triple loops) and verified it on the real 1,200-gene Twist1 network: the influence-partition identity holds to a maximum relative deviation of 1.99e−15 across all 17 time points, weights normalise to exactly 1.0, and all 168 in-network responses enter the scoring. Quadrature is converged at the default (nt = 16): nt = 4 changes weights by at most 9.48e−03 and nt = 8 by 2.88e−05, with identical top-20s.
