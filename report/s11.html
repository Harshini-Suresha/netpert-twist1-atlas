11. Robustness to realistic data problems

Two realistic data problems occur in the real run itself: 14/182 responses are missing from IID+TRRUST, and directed TRRUST edges could be wrong. Dropping all 531 subnetwork TRRUST edges moves ranks by rho = 0.945 (top-20 16/20) — regulatory direction matters, but the ranking survives its removal. The TRRUST directions here are real annotations, not random: this ablation is a genuine test of their contribution. Sign of regulation is still ignored throughout.
