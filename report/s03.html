3. Benchmark against centrality baselines

On the real 1,200-gene Twist1 subnetwork there is no ground truth, so methods are compared by agreement (table below, all 1,200 genes). NetPert tracks betweenness (rho 0.58–0.59) and most closely driver personalised PageRank (rho 0.79), while raw out-degree agrees least (rho 0.51, top-20 overlap only 2/20).
