7. Further analyses

These checks use only the real Twist1 network, so they describe real behaviour, not biology. They include a driver-aware baseline (personalised PageRank from Twist1), the strongest agreement with NetPert (rho 0.79).

Takeaway. On the real network NetPert's top ranks are all direct driver–response connectors, it agrees with betweenness yet is more robust to missing edges, and directed regulatory edges measurably contribute. The dissemination assays cannot confirm or refute this (n = 18, responses only).
